At the foot of Asama.

Forests and mountain scenery shape Kita-Karuizawa and Tsumagoi. Every season brings a different light, a new palette and another way to enjoy the outdoors.

Getting here

Please discuss transport and arrival plans with us before you travel. Detailed directions are provided individually. In winter, plan for snow and icy roads.
