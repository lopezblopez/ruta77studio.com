07 / FAQ

Before you book.

Is there an extra charge for children?+

No. RUTA77 does not apply an additional guest surcharge to children.

Is Wi-Fi available?+

Yes. RUTA77 uses Starlink for internet access, suitable for normal browsing, streaming and online work.

Can I stay in winter?+

Yes. The house has two large Dainichi kerosene fan heaters. Winter driving in the area requires preparation for snow and frozen roads.

Is there a washer or dryer?+

Not at the moment. The house is initially focused on short stays, and we will review the need later.

Are pets allowed?+

No. RUTA77 does not accept pets.

How many people can stay?+

Eight futon sets are available. The final accepted occupancy will follow the completed residential lodging registration and the bedding configuration confirmed for each booking.

HOUSE RULES

House rules

Quiet hours+

Please keep quiet from 9 p.m. During the day, avoid shouting and loud music outdoors. Parties and noisy gatherings are not permitted.

Guests and minors+

Only guests included in the booking may enter the property. Please notify us of any guest changes before arrival. Minors must be accompanied by an adult; unaccompanied minors cannot stay.

Barbecue and fire+

Use only the barbecue provided at the house and follow its instructions. Bringing your own barbecue, lighting bonfires and using fireworks are not permitted.

Smoking+

Smoking indoors, including electronic cigarettes, is prohibited. Smoking is allowed only in the designated outdoor area with an ashtray. Extinguish cigarette ends completely and place them in the ashtray.

Pets+

Pets are not allowed. If you travel with an assistance dog, please contact us before your stay to arrange your arrival.

Property access+

You may use the areas of the property intended for guests. Respect the property boundaries and do not enter neighbouring properties.

Forest setting+

The house is in a forest, so insects may be present, including indoors. Keep doors closed and use insect screens where available. Do not leave food or rubbish outside.

Security cameras+

The property has outdoor security cameras.
