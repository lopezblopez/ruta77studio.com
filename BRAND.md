# RUTA77 — Brand System

## Brand
**Primary name:** RUTA77  
**Descriptor:** Japanese Mountain House · Tsumagoi  
**Secondary / legacy descriptor:** Ruta77 Studio

RUTA77 is a private Japanese mountain house in the forest at the foot of Mt. Asama, in Tsumagoi, Gunma.

## Positioning
RUTA77 is not positioned as a luxury villa and not as a budget cottage. It is an authentic, warm, well-equipped mountain house for families and small groups who want privacy, nature and time together.

## Core promise
**A private house in the forest where guests can get their own time back.**

Japanese:
> 森の中で、時間を取り戻す。

English:
> Find your time in the forest.

Spanish:
> Recupera tu tiempo en el bosque.

## Brand pillars
1. **Forest** — nature is not decoration; it is the setting.
2. **Japan** — wood, tatami, futons and quiet materiality.
3. **Warmth** — physically warm in winter and visually warm all year.
4. **Privacy** — one house, one group, digital entry.
5. **Useful comfort** — full kitchen, Starlink, BBQ, heating and practical equipment.

## Tone of voice
- Calm, specific and confident.
- Never exaggerated.
- Avoid generic luxury language such as “exclusive”, “ultimate”, “opulent” or “five-star”.
- Prefer concrete facts over hype.
- Keep copy short enough to let photography carry the experience.

## Visual system
### Primary colours
- Forest Ink — `#17231E`
- Rice Paper — `#F5F1E8`
- Warm White — `#FFFDF8`
- Volcanic Clay — `#A8573B`
- Tatami / Moss — `#667565`
- Cedar — `#7A624C`

### Typography
Use system fonts to keep the site fast and robust.
- Display / editorial: Georgia, Yu Mincho, Hiragino Mincho ProN
- Interface / body: Arial, Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN, Yu Gothic, Meiryo

### Wordmark
The public-facing wordmark is **RUTA77**. The “77” may use Volcanic Clay as the accent. “Studio” should not dominate the identity.

## Photography direction
1. Real photographs only for the property listing and website.
2. First image: the house and its relationship to the forest.
3. Warm natural light; avoid cold blue interior light.
4. Sequence: exterior → deck → living → tatami → kitchen → sleeping → details → surroundings.
5. Avoid over-processing, fake skies and HDR-heavy edits.
6. Show enough practical detail that the guest understands the product.
7. People, if used, should demonstrate scale and experience rather than pose for the camera.

## Product facts to communicate
- Private whole house.
- Two sleeping areas.
- Up to eight futon sets available; accepted occupancy follows final registration and booking configuration.
- Full kitchen.
- Digital self check-in.
- One private parking space.
- Starlink Wi-Fi.
- Netflix-ready TV.
- Wooden deck.
- BBQ.
- Air conditioning.
- Two large Dainichi kerosene fan heaters for winter.
- No washer/dryer at launch.
- No pets.
- Children do not pay an additional guest surcharge.

## Website objective
The website should answer, in this order:
1. Where am I?
2. What does it feel like?
3. What do I get?
4. What does it cost?
5. Is it right for my group?
6. How do I enquire / book?

## Non-negotiables
- No connection to the owner’s chef work.
- No owner portrait or personal-name-led branding.
- No pet positioning.
- No hourly coworking positioning.
- Do not publish the exact property address before a confirmed booking.
